A licence key gives access to the products covered by that key. Your username and password sign you into your account; your key adds product access to it.

## Add a key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to your account">Open **Your products**. If you're using a trial, sign out of the trial and sign in to your account first.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter the key">Choose **Add licence key**, paste the complete key and choose **Add to my account**.</Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">On success, the loader returns to **Your products** and shows **Licence key added**. Your product list and access are refreshed.</Step>
</Steps>

## Existing keys and renewals

You can add a new key or an eligible existing key already bound to your PC. The server checks whether the key can be linked to your account.

When you redeem a renewal, matching subscriptions can extend your access. A key covering another product can add that product to your library. Check the access shown on each product card after redemption.

A key that belongs to another account cannot be moved by redeeming it again. Contact a seller if your purchase or account needs a review.

## Lifetime access

A covered product displays **Lifetime access** instead of an expiry date. Lifetime access applies to the subscriptions included with the key.

If the products are already covered for life, another key for those same subscriptions may return **Already covered for life**, because it would not add any time.

## Master access

A key with the **Master** subscription covers all current products and future products automatically while that subscription remains active. Product availability and maintenance status still apply.

## Key rejected

The loader keeps you on the key-entry screen when a redemption request fails. Read the popup, correct the issue and try again.
