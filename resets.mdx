title: "Account and hardware resets"
description: "Contact a seller to handle a reset through the bot."
---

**Contact a seller for any account or hardware reset.** A seller needs to handle the reset through the bot. Reinstalling the loader or creating another account is not a hardware reset.

## Hardware reset

If the loader shows **This PC isn't linked**, the account or licence is associated with a different device.

Contact a seller and explain whether you've changed PC or replaced hardware. Have your username and proof of purchase ready so they can review the request and perform an eligible reset through the bot.

Wait for the seller to confirm the reset before signing in again on your current PC.

## Forgotten username or password

Contact a seller so they can help you recover the account through the bot. If a password change is needed, the seller can issue a one-use reset code.

Once you have that code:

1. Open the loader and choose **Forgot details?**.
2. Choose **I have a code**.
3. Enter your username, the code supplied by your seller and a new password.
4. Choose **Reset password**.
5. Sign in with your new password when the loader confirms the change.

The code can only be used once. It has no time limit. Your new password must meet the [account password requirements](/accounts).

## A reset was not accepted

Check the username and code. If the code was already used or you still can't complete the reset, contact the seller who issued it so they can check it through the bot.
