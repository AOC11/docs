title: "One-hour trial"
description: "Check availability, resume your trial and move to paid access."
---

If your PC is eligible, the sign-in screen shows **Redeem 1-hour trial**. The server decides whether a trial is available for your device.

## Start or resume a trial

Choose **Redeem 1-hour trial** when the option is available. If you already have a trial that can continue, the loader may show **Resume 1-hour trial** instead.

The sidebar shows **Trial time left** while the trial is active.

<Note>The one-hour timer continues while the loader is closed. Closing the loader does not pause the trial.</Note>

## After the trial

To use a paid key:

1. Sign out of the trial.
2. Sign in to your account, or create one.
3. Choose **Add licence key** on **Your products**.
4. Redeem the key to update your access.

A trial cannot be restarted by creating another account. If you see **Trial already used** or **Trial unavailable for this PC**, read [access errors](/troubleshooting/access).
