Your account holds the licence keys you add and the product access they provide. Creating an account does not give you paid product access by itself.

## Create an account

1. Open the loader and choose **Create account**.
2. Enter a username and password, then repeat the password.
3. Check that the requirements below the fields are satisfied.
4. Choose **Create account** and wait for the confirmation.
5. Add your licence key from **Your products**.

| Field | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Username | 3–32 characters: letters, numbers, dots, underscores or hyphens. |
| Password | At least five letters, including a capital letter, plus a number and a special character. |
| Confirm password | Must match the password you entered. |

## Sign in

Enter your username and password, then choose **Continue**. The loader checks your account and product access before opening your product list.

**Remember account on this PC** saves the account details for future sign-ins on that PC. Use **Sign out** when you want to leave the current account.

## Forgot your details?

Contact a seller. They can handle account recovery and resets through the bot. If they issue a password reset code, use it in **Forgot details? → I have a code**.

[Read the reset instructions](/resets).

## Account not accepted

If you see **Couldn't sign you in** or **Account couldn't be created**, read [account errors](/troubleshooting/sign-in).
