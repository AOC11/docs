---
title: "Get started"
description: "From your first sign-in to your product list."
---

You need the current Goliath loader, an internet connection and a licence key for paid access. If a trial is available for your PC, you can use the trial option instead.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the current loader">
    [Download the Goliath loader](https://pub-23071c18749e46c5b7fd6f3015559b84.r2.dev/Goliath%20Loader%20V2%20-%20Shipping.exe), then open it. If an update prompt appears, choose **Update Now** and let the loader reopen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an account or sign in">
    Choose **Create account** if this is your first time. Otherwise, enter your username and password and choose **Continue**.

    [Account requirements](/accounts) explain what you need for a new username and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your licence key">
    On **Your products**, choose **Add licence key**, paste your key and choose **Add to my account**.

    When the key is accepted, the loader returns to **Your products** and shows **Licence key added**. Choose **Continue** to close the confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your product">
    Find your product card and check its access, status and version. **Updating / Down** means the product is temporarily unavailable.

    See [Your products](/products) for the card details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Something didn't work?

Read the popup's explanation and **Next step**. The [troubleshooting guide](/troubleshooting/index) lists the messages shown by the loader.
