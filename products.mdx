**Your products** brings your product list and access details together. You do not need a separate Subscriptions tab.

## What each card shows

| Detail | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Product name and image | The product the card represents. |
| Access | **Lifetime access** or the end date of your current access. |
| Status | The current status set for the product. |
| Version | The product's published **Menu updates** version. |
| Last update | The date of the latest DLL upload. |

Read [product status](/product-status) for the three status labels, or [versions and updates](/updates) for the difference between product and loader updates.

## After adding a key

A successful redemption returns you here and refreshes your access. Choose **Continue** on the confirmation to see the list.

## A product is missing

Check that you're signed in to the account holding the correct key. The key must cover the product, and the product must be available to your account.

If your purchase should include it, contact a seller to check the key and product access. Adding the same key repeatedly will not resolve a missing entitlement.

## No products available yet

Choose **Add licence key** to add your paid access. If you already added one, read the error popup or [access troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/access).
