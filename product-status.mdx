---
title: "Product status"
description: "Understand Undetected, Use At Own Risk and Updating / Down."
---

The status badge on each product card shows the status set by the team. Check it before choosing a product.

| Status | Colour | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Undetected** | Green | The team currently lists the product as undetected. |
| **Use At Own Risk** | Orange | The team has flagged the product for caution. |
| **Updating / Down** | Red | The product is paused while the team works on it. Selection is disabled. |

## While a product is updating

The card shows **Updating** in place of **Select**, together with this message:

> We're working on an update. Please check back soon.

Wait until the product becomes available again. Redeeming another key does not remove an updating status.

## Status and access are different

Your access tells you whether your account has the subscription needed for a product. Its status tells you whether that product is currently available or flagged for caution.

A green status does not change your licence expiry or guarantee that a product is risk-free.
