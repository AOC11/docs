---
title: "Licence key errors"
description: "Understand why a key was not accepted."
---

## Licence key not found

**Support code:** `invalid_key`

The server could not find an active record for the key you entered.

**Next step:** Copy the complete key from your purchase details. If it is still rejected, contact your seller.

## Licence key not found

**Support code:** `redeem_invalid`

The key you tried to add was not recognised by the server.

**Next step:** Copy the complete key from your purchase details, then try Add licence key again.

## This key can't be linked

**Support code:** `redeem_used`

This key is already in use or is not eligible to be linked to this account.

**Next step:** Sign in to the account the key belongs to. If you're unsure, contact your seller with proof of purchase.

## Key already on this account

**Support code:** `redeem_self`

You're trying to add the same key that is already active on this account.

**Next step:** Enter a different key to extend your access or add another subscription.

## Paid account required

**Support code:** `redeem_trial`

A trial key or trial session cannot be used for this key-linking request.

**Next step:** Sign out of the trial, sign in to your account, then add your paid licence key.

## Already covered for life

**Support code:** `redeem_no_change`

These subscriptions already have lifetime access, so this key would not add more time.

**Next step:** You can keep using your current subscriptions. Ask your seller if you expected a different product.

## Your key needs a review

**Support code:** `review_required`

This licence is on hold until its details have been reviewed.

**Next step:** Contact your seller with your purchase details so they can check the licence.
