---
title: "Product and access errors"
description: "Check hardware matching, subscriptions, product availability and trials."
---

## This PC isn't linked

**Support code:** `hwid_mismatch`

This account or licence is linked to a different PC. Your current device does not match its hardware ID.

**Next step:** Use the original PC, or contact your seller to review a hardware reset if you've changed your device.

**Hardware reset:** Contact a seller so they can review and handle the reset through the bot. See [reset help](/resets).

## No matching subscription

**Support code:** `subscription_missing`

Your account or key does not include an active subscription for this product.

**Next step:** Add a key for the product you want, or ask your seller to check your subscriptions.

## Your access has expired

**Support code:** `subscription_expired`

The subscription needed for this request has reached its end date.

**Next step:** Add a new licence key or renew through your seller, then sign in again.

## Subscription paused

**Support code:** `subscription_paused`

The subscription needed for this request is currently paused.

**Next step:** Check for service updates or contact your seller to find out when access will resume.

## Product unavailable

**Support code:** `product_unavailable`

This product is not available right now. It may be updating or temporarily disabled.

**Next step:** Check the product status and try again later. Contact your seller if the issue continues.

## Access has been blocked

**Support code:** `banned`

The server has blocked this account, licence or device from accessing the service.

**Next step:** Contact your seller or support team to request a review. Repeated sign-in attempts will not remove the block.

## Trial already used

**Support code:** `trial_used`

This PC has already used its one-hour trial.

**Next step:** Sign in to an account and add a paid licence key to continue using the products.

## Trial unavailable for this PC

**Support code:** `trial_ineligible`

The server has confirmed that this PC is not eligible for a one-hour trial.

**Next step:** Use a paid licence, or contact your seller if you think the eligibility result is incorrect.

## No trial to resume

**Support code:** `trial_not_started`

There is no active trial available to resume on this PC.

**Next step:** Start a trial if the option is available, or sign in with your paid account.

## Account access not confirmed

**Support code:** `account_unverified`

The server could not confirm that this account is ready for licence access.

**Next step:** Sign in again. If the message returns, ask your seller to review the account.

## Product not confirmed

The selected product was not confirmed by the server.

**Next step:** Return to Your products and try again. Contact your seller if this continues.

## Product setup needs a review

The product has conflicting configuration details.

**Next step:** Contact your seller so they can check the product setup.

## Product setup is incomplete

This product is missing a required configuration setting.

**Next step:** Contact your seller so they can complete the product setup.

## Product setup needs a review

A required product configuration setting is invalid.

**Next step:** Contact your seller so they can check the product setup.

## Your product access changed

The selected product is no longer included in your current access.

**Next step:** Check Your products, or contact your seller if you expected to keep access.

## Product couldn't be prepared

The loader could not finish preparing this product.

**Next step:** Try again. If the problem continues, contact your seller.

## Product download incomplete

The server returned no usable product file.

**Next step:** Try again. If the problem continues, contact your seller.
