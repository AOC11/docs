title: "Sign-in and account errors"
description: "Find the next step for sign-in, registration and password reset messages."
---

The server uses `account_invalid` for several account requests. Match the **popup title** to the action you were taking.

## Account couldn't be created

**Support code:** `account_invalid`

The username may already be in use, or your details did not meet the account requirements.

**Next step:** Choose another username and check the password requirements. If you already have an account, sign in.

## Password reset not accepted

**Support code:** `account_invalid`

The username, reset code or new password was not accepted. Reset codes can only be used once.

**Next step:** Check the username and code from your seller, then enter a password that meets the requirements.

## Couldn't sign you in

**Support code:** `account_invalid`

The username or password was not accepted.

**Next step:** Check your details and try again. Use Forgot your details? if you need help recovering your account.

## Ask a seller for a reset

Account and password resets need to be handled by a seller through the bot. Use **Forgot details? → I have a code** only after a seller has issued your code. [See reset instructions](/resets).
