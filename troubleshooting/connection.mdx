---
title: "Connection and update errors"
description: "Find the next step for connection, session and update messages."
---

## Please sign in again

**Support code:** `session_invalid`

Your session has ended or is no longer accepted by the server.

**Next step:** Sign out, then sign in again to refresh your account and product access.

## Your account has changed

**Support code:** `account_changed`

Your account details changed while this request was being processed.

**Next step:** Sign out and sign in again before retrying the request.

## Please wait a moment

**Support code:** `rate_limited`

Too many requests have been made in a short period of time.

**Next step:** Wait a minute before trying again. Repeated attempts may keep the temporary limit active.

## A loader update is required

**Support code:** `loader_update_required`

This loader build is not currently approved by the server.

**Next step:** Use Check for updates or download the latest approved loader from your seller.

## Service temporarily unavailable

**Support code:** `service_unavailable`

The server could not complete the request right now.

**Next step:** Try again shortly. If the problem continues, check for service updates or contact your seller.

## Couldn't connect

The loader could not complete a secure connection to the server.

**Next step:** Check your internet connection and try again. If other websites work, the service may be temporarily unavailable.

## Please sign in again

Your session has expired. Product access needs to be confirmed again.

**Next step:** Sign out and sign in again, then retry your request.

## Please wait a moment

The server is temporarily limiting requests.

**Next step:** Wait a minute before trying again.

## Response couldn't be verified

The loader could not verify this request safely, so access was not confirmed.

**Next step:** Reopen the latest approved loader and try again. Contact your seller if the message returns.

## Request couldn't be completed

The loader could not confirm this request. No successful result was received.

**Next step:** Check your details and connection, then try again. If it continues, copy these details and contact your seller.
