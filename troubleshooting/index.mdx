## Use the details in the popup

Choose **Copy details** to copy the title, explanation, next step and support code, when one is shown. If you contact a seller, these details help them identify the request that failed.

Choose **Got it** or press **Escape** to close the popup. A success popup uses **Continue**.

## Need a reset?

Contact a seller so they can handle the reset through the bot. [Account and hardware resets](/resets) explains the process.
