---
title: "Frequently asked questions"
description: "Quick answers about your account, access and updates."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where did the Subscriptions tab go?">
    Access is shown on **Your products**. Each product card includes lifetime or dated access, alongside status, version and last update.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I go after redeeming a key?">
    The loader returns to **Your products** with your updated access. Choose **Continue** on the success popup.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add a paid key while using a trial?">
    Sign out of the trial first, sign in to your account, then choose **Add licence key**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does closing the loader pause my trial?">
    No. The one-hour timer continues while the loader is closed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does lifetime access cover every product?">
    Lifetime applies to the subscriptions included with your key. **Master** access covers all current and future products while the Master subscription is active.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I select an Updating / Down product?">
    The team has paused that product while working on it. Wait until it is available again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I reset my hardware ID myself?">
    Contact a seller. They need to review and handle the hardware reset through the bot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do the loader and product show different versions?">
    They are separate releases. The bottom of the window shows the loader version; each product card shows its published Menu updates version.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
